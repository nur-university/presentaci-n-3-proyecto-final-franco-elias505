Solar for Schools

Vision

Our vision is to bring clean, renewable energy to schools across the region, reducing carbon footprints and energy costs while providing educational opportunities for students to learn about sustainable energy solutions.

Impact

This project will install solar panels on 10 local schools, providing clean energy for over 5,000 students. The installation will reduce each school's energy costs by up to 80%, allowing more resources to be allocated to educational programs and materials.

Students will have the opportunity to learn about renewable energy through hands-on experiences and educational programs integrated into the curriculum. The solar installations will serve as living laboratories for science, technology, and environmental education.

Budget Breakdown

  • Solar Panels & Equipment: $12,000 (60%)
  • Installation & Labor: $5,000 (25%)
  • Educational Materials: $2,000 (10%)
  • Maintenance Fund: $1,000 (5%)
